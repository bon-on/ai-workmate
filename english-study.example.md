# Daily English Study Messages

Copy this file to `english-study-weekday-hourly.md` and add a `## <Weekday>` section with
`### HH:MM` lessons for each slot you schedule. Lines must keep the labels below.

## Monday

### 12:00
Focus: Ordering at a cafe
Vocabulary (5): order, receipt, table, menu, card
Core pattern: I'd like to ...
Mini reading: Mina orders a sandwich and an iced coffee, then pays by card.
Example sentences:
- I'd like to order a sandwich and an iced coffee.
- Can I see the menu, please?
- I'll pay by card.
- Could I get a receipt?
- Let's find a table by the window.
Speaking task: Order your usual lunch out loud in three sentences.
Writing challenge: Write two sentences about your favorite cafe.
